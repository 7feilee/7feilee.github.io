Abstract. Ten years of security engineering across NEC Laboratories Europe, Alibaba, and NIO. I work on systems whose guarantees hold without trusting the operator — multi-party computation, homomorphic encryption, trusted execution, federated learning — and on the operational side of security at enterprise scale. Two IEEE publications. Currently building Mayfly, an EU AI Act compliance advisory that uses privacy-enhancing technologies as the bridge between Chinese AI companies and European regulation.

Premise. Trust is a property to be verified, not a feeling to be marketed. Accordingly this page is plain HTML — no scripts, no analytics, no cookies, no third-party requests — and each claim below links to something you can check.

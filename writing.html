Writing

Nine wikis · 200+ chapters · Chinese, with English terms

Method. Each wiki is an attempt to map one domain end to end — ownership, incentives, mechanisms — rather than to opine on it. All are open-source static sites on GitHub Pages: plain Markdown, public-domain illustrations, verifiable sources, no paywall. Figures age; each site says so on every page. Corrections are welcome as issues on GitHub.

1.Political economy

№WikiContents
W1 AI 工业大爆炸ai-industrial-explosion The physical value chain of AI — chips, HBM memory, power, data centers; how sector profit pools are being redrawn; what is good and bad for VCs and retail investors; which jobs and businesses survive. 21 chapters.
W2 资本有国界吗？capital-atlas A typology and atlas of global capital — sovereign funds, asset managers, PE/VC, family fortunes, crypto and stablecoins — by country and by kind, ending on whether capital has borders. 36 chapters.
W3 媒体权力图谱media-power-atlas Who owns the media, what funds it, how left/right ratings are made (and contested), and how AI chatbots became the new gatekeepers. Strictly neutral; ratings attributed to third parties. 24 chapters.
W4 你没见过的东西business-insights A handbook of recurring business mechanics — twelve “mother patterns” that keep reappearing beneath different industries. 29 chapters and a glossary.

About

Qifei Li

Security Engineer · Privacy Computing Researcher · Heidelberg, Germany

01

Professional Journey

I'm a security engineer with over 10 years of experience spanning Europe and Asia. Most recently I led information security at NIO Inc. in Munich, where I transformed security operations through SIEM modernization and automation — achieving a 60% cost reduction and 80% improvement in mean time to response.

02

Research & Privacy Computing

My research focuses on privacy-enhancing technologies that let computation cross borders without data crossing borders. At NEC Labs Europe I built SGX-based secure AI inference systems. At Alibaba I pioneered HomoPAI — a production platform for privacy-preserving machine learning published at IEEE ICDE 2020.

I founded Mayfly to apply this research commercially: helping Chinese AI companies navigate EU AI Act compliance using PET (MPC, FL, TEE, HE) as the technical bridge across regulatory jurisdictions.
